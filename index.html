Qianli Ma

I am a Senior Research Scientist at NVIDIA Cosmos Lab, working on large-scale multimodal world models. My work spans model architecture, pre- and mid-training, and diffusion distillation, with a current focus on real-time, interactive generation.

I have been a core contributor to every generation of NVIDIA's Cosmos world models, and currently lead technical efforts in distillation and interactive simulation.

My earlier research focused on 3D representations, reconstruction, and human modeling. I received my Bachelor's in physics from Peking University and my PhD from the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems and ETH Zürich, advised by Michael Black and Siyu Tang.
